Customer Relationship Management (CRM) System using Agile


1. Abstract 

Customer Relationship Management (CRM) is an important system used by organizations to manage interactions with customers, store customer data, and improve customer satisfaction. In many small and medium organizations, customer information is handled manually using paper records or basic spreadsheets. This leads to problems such as data loss, duplication, slow access to information, and poor customer service. To overcome these issues, a computerized CRM system is required.

This project focuses on developing a Customer Relationship Management system using Agile methodology. The proposed system helps in storing customer details such as name, contact information, communication history, and service requests in a structured digital format. The system allows employees to quickly access customer information, track interactions, manage complaints, and generate reports. Agile methodology is used to ensure flexible development, continuous improvement, and user involvement throughout the project lifecycle.

The project is divided into small development cycles called sprints. Each sprint delivers a working module of the system, such as customer registration, interaction tracking, or complaint handling. Regular feedback is taken from users after each sprint, and changes are implemented accordingly. This approach reduces development risk and improves software quality.
