Travel Booking Platform using Agile


1. Abstract 

A Travel Booking Platform is an online system that allows users to search, book, and manage travel-related services such as flights, hotels, and travel packages from a single platform. In traditional travel booking systems, customers depend on travel agents or physical offices, which makes the process time-consuming, costly, and inconvenient. With the rapid growth of internet usage and digital services, there is a strong need for an automated and user-friendly travel booking solution.

This project aims to develop a Travel Booking Platform using Agile methodology. The proposed system enables users to register, search for available travel options, compare prices, book tickets or hotel rooms, and make secure online payments. The system also allows users to view booking history and cancel bookings if required. From the administrator side, the system helps manage travel listings, bookings, and customer information efficiently.
