Healthcare Management System using Agile

1. Abstract 

A Healthcare Management System is a software application designed to manage hospital and clinic operations such as patient registration, appointment scheduling, medical records, and billing. In many healthcare institutions, patient data is still handled manually using paper files or basic digital records, which can lead to data loss, errors, and delays in treatment. To overcome these challenges, an automated healthcare management system is required.

This project focuses on developing a Healthcare Management System using Agile methodology. The proposed system helps in maintaining patient details, doctor information, appointment schedules, treatment records, and billing information in a structured and secure manner. Patients can register, book appointments, and view their medical details, while hospital staff can manage records efficiently.
